About Us

Transforming African Agriculture from Reactive to Predictive

With a passion for empowering farming communities, we work across regions to implement practical solutions that improve productivity, enhance resilience, and promote environmental stewardship.

THE AGROLINK STORY

Africa grows enough food. Yet millions still go hungry. 30–50% of harvest is lost before reaching the market. The problem isn't production—it's connection. Farmers lack market intelligence, supply chains are disconnected, and climate risks go undetected. Agriculture needs an intelligent ecosystem—not isolated solutions.

Introducing Agrolink Africa: an AI-powered platform that connects the entire agricultural ecosystem and predicts decisions before they happen. We are building a platform where AI collects data, predicts demand and risks, and sends smart recommendations to farmers while connecting them directly to buyers and transport systems.

Our implementation strategy rolls out across 3 phases: Months 1–6 MVP launch in Kiambu, Nakuru, and Meru; Months 7–18 National Kenya expansion with AgroMarket and AgriFinance; Years 2–3 Regional East African expansion. By Year Three, we aim to onboard 300,000 farmers, increase farmer net income by 30%, reduce post-harvest waste by 40%, and train 50,000 farmers in digital climate-smart practices.
