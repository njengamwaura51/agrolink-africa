How It Works: Connected End-to-End

Agrolink unites the entire value chain: AI collects data, predicts demand and risks, and sends smart recommendations while connecting farmers directly to buyers and transport systems:

01
Intelligent Demand & Advisory

Satellite telemetry, soil indicators, and real-time market demand forecasting delivered to farmers before planting.

02
Omnichannel Market Matching

Farmers log harvests via basic 2G USSD (*384*254#) or Web app; verified supermarkets and distributors bid directly.

03
Cold Chain & Escrow Settlement

Automated fleet routing picks up produce at collection hubs; M-Pesa escrow guarantees immediate mobile money payout.
